The idea behind the board

Local life works better when we're in the loop.

Mohalla is being designed as a simple, city-aware place for people to share useful neighbourhood information — without needing to know the right group chat first.

◎

Grounded in place

Discover conversations by city and neighbourhood, not an endless global feed.

↗

Useful by design

Local questions, civic issues, events, alerts and everyday recommendations have a home.

♡

Respectful by default

Privacy, clarity and community guidelines should be part of the experience from day one.

How it works

Choose a city, browse local updates, search by keyword or type, and share a useful post. The current website is a front-end prototype with illustrative sample content. New demo posts are stored only in the browser where they are created.

What comes next

Account creation, sign-in, a shared database, verified moderation workflows, reporting review, and reliable cross-device data are planned work. They are not active yet, so this prototype should not be treated as a live community service.

Sample names, posts, event details and activity counts are fictional or illustrative. They are not endorsements, official notices or verified reports.
