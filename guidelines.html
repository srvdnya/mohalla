A good neighbour starts here

Community guidelines.

Keep Mohalla useful, welcoming and safe for the people who share your neighbourhood.

Be useful and specific

Use clear titles, include relevant details, and distinguish what you know from what you suspect. Add dates and locations only as precisely as needed.

Respect people's privacy

  • Don't post private phone numbers, home addresses, identity documents or private conversations without permission.
  • Never publish someone's precise location or personal details to shame, threaten or target them.
  • Ask before sharing identifiable photos of other people.

Don't spread unverified claims

For public safety, health, transit or civic alerts, link to an official source where possible. Avoid presenting rumours as facts. The current prototype does not verify posts.

Be kind; disagree constructively

No harassment, hate, threats, intimidation, impersonation or repeated unwanted contact. Criticise an issue or decision without targeting a person.

Use the right channel for emergencies

Mohalla is not an emergency service and demo alerts are not live or verified. Contact the relevant local emergency service for urgent help.

Reporting and moderation are not yet connected to a review team in this prototype. These guidelines describe the intended community standard, not a claim that all protections are live.
