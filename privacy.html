Privacy matters

Privacy overview.

A clear explanation of what this prototype does today — and what is not implemented yet.

This is prototype information, not a final legal privacy policy. A complete policy must be reviewed before a public production launch.

What is stored in this prototype

Demo posts you create, your selected city and upvote markers may be saved in your browser's local storage. That data is not automatically shared with other visitors and may disappear if you clear site data or use another browser or device.

Location

The current multi-page preview lets you enter a city manually. It does not need precise GPS coordinates. Do not enter sensitive personal details into sample forms.

Accounts and analytics

Sign-in, account creation, cross-device profiles, a production database and a moderation system are not implemented. This site should not be treated as a finished service.

Before launch

Mohalla will need a reviewed privacy policy, data retention and deletion controls, secure authentication, abuse prevention, and clear consent for any location-related features before it is ready for public production use.
